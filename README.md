A 21-person pharma IT team went from idea to working apps in one afternoon. ContactMonkey's first generative AI feature went from zero to production in 10 weeks.

That is the job: get one AI tool into daily use, fast, and prove what it changed. I work with operating partners and portfolio CEOs whose AI pilots never reached the business.

## Most pilots fail on waiting, not on the model

In 1990, an insurer took 22 days to process an application that needed 17 minutes of work. The rest was queues and handoffs. A faster model only speeds up the 17 minutes.

So I start with the waiting. I map one workflow, delete the waste, build what is left and measure it. Your board gets the before and after.

## One tool live in 4 weeks

This is the standard first engagement: one workflow, with one owner on your side. You get five things.

1. **The workflow, mapped from your logs.** Hours of work and days of waiting on every step.
2. **The tool in production.** It runs inside the systems your team already uses. No new screen, no migration.
3. **A one-page board readout.** Before and after numbers that your board and a future buyer can check.
4. **A runbook and a handover.** Your team runs and changes the tool without me.
5. **Your next five workflows, ranked.** Each one shows what it costs you to leave it as it is.

I write plain code first and use agents only where judgment pays. Every step that moves money or reaches a customer waits for a person. Each tool is tested on cases with known answers, then measured again at 30 days.
